Galaxy International Convention Center

Galaxy International Convention Center (GICC) is the latest addition to Galaxy Entertainment Group’s ever-expanding integrated resort precinct and will usher in a new era for the MICE industry in Macau.

GICC — Asia's most iconic and advanced MICE destination — is a world-class event venue featuring 40,000 sqm of total flexible MICE space that can cater for up to 8,500 banquet guests, or cocktail parties for as many as 16,000 guests. The 10,000-sqm pillar-less exhibition hall located on the ground floor, can accommodate up to 7,000 delegates. The exhibition hall is also perfectly suited for banquets of approximately 6,500 guests or cocktail parties for 8,500 guests.

Conveniently located within GICC, the 16,000-seat Galaxy Arena is the largest indoor arena in Macau and where spectacular events come to life - from world concert tours to thrilling fight nights, every guest at Galaxy Arena will enjoy 360°unobstructed views and high quality surround sound while high definition broadcasting will bring the event to every corner of the arena.



Visa

  • For Mainland Chinese: Individuals holding a Chinese Passport, Hong Kong Non-permanent Resident Card, Hong Kong Visa, Landing Slip, and EEP (Exit-Entry Permit) are granted visa‑free entry to Macao for a stay of up to 7 days. It is not recommended to travel to Zhuhai without an EEP visa label.
  • For Hong Kong Permanent Residents: Please use your Hong Kong Permanent Identity Card for immigration clearance.
  • For Foreigners: Foreign nationals should use their valid passports to enter Macao.


Note: Visitors from Bangladesh, Nepal, Nigeria, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, and Vietnam are required to apply for a Macao visa in advance through the Commissioner's Office of China's Foreign Ministry in the Hong Kong SAR (香港外交公署). For more information, please visit: Visa to China and Immigration Clearance of Non-residents of Macao.
